# Mind lake ⛵

**You are the shore. Your thoughts are only boats.**

### 👉 [Try it here: priyanka-senthil.github.io/Mind_Lake](https://priyanka-senthil.github.io/Mind_Lake/)

Mind lake is a calm, 90-second mindfulness game for grown-ups and kids. Inside a human head, a child sits on a quiet shore and watches thoughts sail across a lake as colored boats: kindness, friendship, silly ideas, worries, comparison, self-doubt, and more.
<img width="1267" height="833" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca80fcb4-de05-4f90-a839-129002267664" />

You don't fight your thoughts or chase them. You notice them, take a slow breath, and let them sail out of sight.

## Why try it

- **It takes 90 seconds.** That's a quick reset for a busy day, before school, or at bedtime.
- **It shows how a mind really works.** It has 13 kinds of boats and more than 130 everyday thoughts about friends, family, and ourselves.
- **It teaches breathing.** Heavy thoughts drift toward the shore, and a slow breath carries them away.
- **You can add your own thoughts.** Type what's on your mind and watch it set sail. It stays private on your device.
- **It works anywhere.** It runs in any browser, on phone or desktop, with gentle ocean sound and a night mode.

There's no sign-up, and there's no way to lose. There are only ways to come back to the shore.
